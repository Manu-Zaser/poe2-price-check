              The overlay

              Chips
              Category or this base only, corrupted state, item level, sockets and whether all ticked stats must match. Click to switch, scroll over item level/sockets to change the value.
              Stats with Min / Max
              Tick the stats to search for. Values start at your item's own value – scroll over a field to change it or click to type. Under each mod you see where it comes from (Explicit, Implicit, Augment …) and its tier.
              Weapons
              Below 20% quality, the DPS rows offer Q20: the DPS the weapon would have at 20% quality, as minimum. Elemental DPS lets you pick the element (Any, Cold …).
              Key & Low value
              For rares, only the mods that usually carry the price on that item type are ticked (Key), e.g. movement speed on boots. Low value mods are faded. "ⓘ key mods … not on this item" lists what the item is missing. Adjust both lists under Settings → Mod value.
              Hidden & Collapse
              Mods already counted in a total, the DPS or the defences are hidden; Hidden shows them. Collapse folds the stat list away.
              Search & Trade ↗
              Nothing is searched until you click Search. Trade ↗ opens the same search on pathofexile.com.
              Typical price
              The median of the 3 cheapest offers – the single cheapest offer alone is often mispriced. Old offers count too: they can still be bought.
              Listings
              The 20 cheapest offers. Hover a row to see that item's stats – including DPS for weapons – laid out like the trade site; ✓ marks mods you searched for. Click a row to open that offer on the trade site. The dot in front of the price compares the offer with your item on the stats you ticked: green better, yellow mixed, red worse.

              More hotkeys

              Open on trade site

              The item opens on pathofexile.com/trade in your browser, with the same filters the overlay would use.

              Open in wiki

              The unique – or the item's base – opens on poe2wiki.net.

              Change or turn them off under Settings → Hotkeys.

              Waystones and tablets

              Price-check a waystone or tablet and dangerous mods are marked red, with a warning at the top of the overlay – before you put it into the map device. Which mods count as dangerous is up to you: Settings → Map mods.

              Market

              The Market page lists all poe.ninja prices of the league by category – currency, fragments, runes and soul cores, essences, gems and uniques – with the 7-day trend. Rising / Falling this week shows the biggest movers. Search across all categories, sort by value or change, click an item to show it in the overlay, and ☆ star items to pin them to the watchlist on Home. Badges such as Steady climb, Spike or Sliding describe the 7-day curve (hover for details) – they don't predict prices. Prices update hourly; the page sends no extra requests.

              Good to know

              • Move the overlay by dragging any empty spot – it stays there for the next checks. Close it with × or Esc.
              • Recent checks on the Home page reopen a check in the overlay without searching again.
              • GGG allows only a few trade searches per minute. If you search too often, the overlay asks you to wait a few seconds.
              • The league is chosen automatically – after a league start the tool switches within an hour.
              • Closing the window keeps the tool running in the tray (bottom right, next to the clock). Quit it there with right-click → Quit.

              Mod value

              For rare and magic items, only key mods are searched by default – the price of a rare depends on 2–3 mods, not on all of them. Low value mods are shown faded and never searched by default. One rule per line: slot: text, where text is part of the mod (upper/lower case doesn't matter) and slot is one of weapon caster amulet ring belt boots gloves helmet body shield quiver jewel, for attack weapons also melee ranged bow crossbow, or * for all.
